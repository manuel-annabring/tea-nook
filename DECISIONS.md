# Decisions

Format and rules: `WORKFLOW.md` section 6. Impact `foundation` = other slices build on it (expensive to change). `easy-to-change` = a value or look that can be retuned in minutes. Status is `open` until Manuel has seen it at review, then `accepted` or `changed`.

| ID | Slice | Decision | Alternatives | Why | Impact | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 0.2 | Steam uses a plain `ShaderMaterial` | `onBeforeCompile` on a standard material, drei `shaderMaterial` | Steam is unlit, transparent and needs full control of vertex displacement and alpha; `onBeforeCompile` stays reserved for lit surfaces | foundation | accepted |
| D-002 | 0.2 | Steam geometry is one camera-facing billboard around Y, 16 x 48 segments | open tube, crossed planes | The twist stays visible and it is one draw call; tube hides the twist, crossed planes get thin edge-on. Known limit: collapses when seen from straight above, so the polar minimum stays far from vertical | foundation | accepted |
| D-003 | 0.2 | Noise is a tileable Perlin `DataTexture` (128 x 128) generated at startup | procedural GLSL noise, shipped PNG | One texture fetch per fragment, no asset to load, CPU twin is unit-tested | foundation | accepted |
| D-004 | 0.2 | No `uSpeed` uniform; the CPU accumulates `uPhase += delta * speed` | `uTime * uSpeed` | Damping speed between teas never makes the pattern jump | foundation | accepted |
| D-005 | 0.2 | Pointer reaches the shader through `PointerProbe` (ray-plane on the canvas element, plain numbers in the Zustand store, read with `getState()` in `useFrame`) | invisible hit mesh, store subscription | Never blocks mesh pointer events (the mug click needs them), no re-render per mouse move | foundation | accepted |
| D-006 | 0.2 | `leva` 0.10.1 as dev-only tweak panel, lazy loaded under `import.meta.env.DEV` | hand-written panel | Approved by Manuel; verified absent from the production bundle | easy-to-change | accepted |
| D-007 | 0.2 | Steam defaults tuned by Manuel in the Leva panel on 2026-10-27 (`STEAM_LOOK_DEFAULTS`) | none | Taste decision made live | easy-to-change | accepted |
| D-008 | 0 | Work is organized in phases 0 to 5 (SPEC section 7); phase 2 (live Blender modeling) is collaborative, the rest autonomous | slice-by-slice approval; all phases autonomous | Modeling is taste-driven and needs the live session (`WORKFLOW.md` section 12); the rest can run unattended | foundation | open |
| D-009 | 1.4 (planned) | The app sets contract material colors from `PALETTE` in `src/config/look.ts` by material name after load | colors baked in the GLB only | Palette changes after review take minutes and need no Blender export. Material names stay the contract; only colors are overridden | foundation | open |
| D-010 | 1.3 (planned) | SPEC section 5 sizes and budgets are used unchanged as the working values | change sizes in slice 1.3 | A change would be a contract change and a stop; amendments are made in phase 2 with Manuel present | foundation | accepted |
| D-011 | 0 | Phase order: phase 1, then phase 2 (live Blender) in the same sitting as the review, then phase 3 unattended | phase 3 before phase 2 | Look-defining shapes come before interaction; Manuel is present at review anyway | easy-to-change | accepted |
| D-012 | 0 | Phase 1 starts right after a usage-window reset; extra usage stays off; pause and resume is accepted | enable extra usage | Pro plan, extra usage capped at about 20 EUR per month | easy-to-change | accepted |
| D-013 | 1.1 | `look.ts` re-exports `TEAS`, `STEAM_LOOK_DEFAULTS`, `STEAM_DEFAULTS` from their current files; palette includes placeholder colors for the 7 non-SPEC-section-3 contract materials | moving the data into `look.ts` | Values unchanged, no churn in tested files | easy-to-change | open |
| D-014 | 1.1 | Dev hooks are lazy components (`DevHooks`, `DevPanel`) behind `import.meta.env.DEV`; the steam mesh got `name="Steam"` (one-line edit in `Steam.tsx`, outside the listed scope) so the ready check can find it. `pick` skips transparent materials (steam is displaced in the shader, CPU raycast would be wrong). Ready = preset applied, `Mug` and `Steam` mounted, 2 frames rendered | traverse for a ShaderMaterial; hooks inside Scene | Lazy import is dropped from the production bundle (verified: no `__nook` in dist) | easy-to-change | open |
| D-015 | 1.1 | `OrbitControls` gets `makeDefault`; `?cam` sets camera position and `controls.target`, then `controls.update()` | set only the camera | OrbitControls would otherwise re-aim at its own target | easy-to-change | open |
| D-016 | 1.2 | `Mug` and `Kettle` origin check compares the origin with the center of the underside (xy middle of the vertices within 1 mm of the lowest point, plus the lowest z), not the bounding-box center | bbox center (as in Typewriter's key check) | A spout or handle shifts the bbox center, so a bbox check would reject a correct kettle or mug. `TeaSurface`, `Window_Glass`, `Window_Sky` (symmetric discs and quads) do use the bbox center | foundation | open |
| D-017 | 1.2 | Marker plausibility thresholds: `Steam_Tea` within 30 mm in z of the Mug bbox top and inside its footprint; `Steam_Kettle` inside the kettle bbox grown by 30 mm, in the upper half, and off the kettle axis by at least 40% of the horizontal half extent. Constants live in `contract_spec.py` | tighter or looser numbers; no spout-side direction check | SPEC says only "near the rim" and "near the spout side"; the spout direction is not in the contract. The numbers are tunable and will be revisited when the blockout (1.3) and real kettle (2.2) exist | easy-to-change | open |
| D-018 | 1.2 | `export_glb.py` exports to a scratch directory first, checks the 3 MB budget, then moves the file to `public/models/nook.glb`; an oversize or failed export exits 1 and leaves the target untouched. Also checks: `Room` first UV layer named `UVMap`, at most 14 materials in use, triangle cap 100k is an error and the 30k target a warning (triangles counted on evaluated meshes) | Typewriter's approach (write, then warn about size) | A refused export must write nothing, as the acceptance criteria require | easy-to-change | open |
| D-019 | 1.2 | `render_preview.py` writes `front`, `side`, `top` and `iso`; cameras are framed from the mesh bounds (any scene), the side view looks from +X because the left wall is at -X. `contract.ts` also exports `assertModelContract` (throws with the missing names) and typed `BUDGETS`, `MATERIAL_NAMES`; the twin test parses `contract_spec.py` through a Vite `?raw` import (no `@types/node` needed) | hard-coded camera positions as in Typewriter; reading the file with `node:fs` | Works on the factory cube and later on the real model without edits | easy-to-change | open |
| D-020 | 1.3 | Blockout layout and fill-in numbers, all inside the SPEC section 5 sizes (counter 0.60 x 0.45, walls 0.30, mug 0.09, kettle 0.20): counter 0.04 thick, walls 0.02 thick, window opening 0.22 x 0.16 in the left wall (sill at z 0.10) with 15 mm frame bars, mug at (0, -0.07), kettle at (0.17, 0.11) with the spout pointing to -X, plant back-left, tin front-left, books back-centre, sun patch `Rays_Target` at (-0.10, 0, 0). Mug is hollow (tea disc 6 mm below the rim) with a handle block; kettle has a spout wedge, handle block, lid and knob (`Mat_KettleDetail`) | other layouts; solid mug | The spec gives only the main sizes. Hollow mug so `TeaSurface` is visible; props spread so the window light patch, both steams and the plant read from the front-right camera. All numbers are parameters at the top of `01_blockout.py` and get replaced by real shapes in phase 2 | easy-to-change | open |
| D-021 | 1.3 | `Mat_Glass` is see-through in the file (alpha 0.35, glTF `BLEND`, base colour still the palette value); `Mat_Sky` is opaque. `Window_Sky` is 3 cm larger than the opening on each side (stays inside the wall height) and 5 cm outside the glass | opaque glass | An opaque glass quad would hide the sky card. `look.ts` (D-009) overrides colour only, not alpha, so the alpha has to come from the model. Slice 1.4 may change it at runtime | easy-to-change | open |
| D-022 | 1.3 | `render_preview.py` (slice 1.2 script) `iso` view now looks from the front-right (0.6, -1, 0.7) instead of the front-left. The `front` and `top` orthographic views cannot show the window face (it is in the left wall, edge-on from both); `side` and `iso` do.
